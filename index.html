Official Privacy Policy

PaisaTrack – Smart Expense & Budget Tracker

Effective Date: October 11, 2026  |  Package ID: com.sonu.PaisaTrack.app  |  Version: 1.0.0

Welcome to PaisaTrack ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Sonu Lal. Your financial privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how PaisaTrack handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary at a Glance:
  • 100% On-Device Storage: PaisaTrack operates completely offline using local SQLite Room Database on your device.
  • Zero Tracking: No user telemetry, hidden analytics, or behavioral tracking.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to provide an email, phone number, or sign-up to use PaisaTrack.
  • Complete Data Ownership: Your financial records remain strictly on your physical phone.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or financial data on external servers. All data you enter into the app—including income entries, expense records, notes, categories, currency preferences, and monthly budgets—is stored exclusively locally on your device using Android's secure internal storage (SQLite Room Database).

2. Permissions Used by the App

PaisaTrack follows a strict least-privilege policy and does not request any intrusive permissions:

  • No Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Location Access: We never access your personal media, contacts, or GPS location.
  • Local Storage / Clipboard (Optional): Only used when you manually choose to export or restore your own JSON data backup.

3. Data Backup, Export & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your records at all times:

  • You can delete individual transactions or reset budgets directly inside the app.
  • You can manually export a JSON backup to your clipboard or restore from a JSON backup in the Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage from your Android device settings will permanently delete all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

PaisaTrack does not integrate third-party advertising networks or data brokers. The "About & Developer" section includes optional external links to the developer's official social media profiles (YouTube, Instagram, Snapchat) and support email. Clicking these links will open the respective external platform at your discretion.

5. Children's Privacy (PEGI 3 / Everyone)

PaisaTrack is a general-audience personal finance and budgeting utility suitable for all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any user of any age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected both inside the app's Privacy Policy dialog and on this page with an updated effective date.
